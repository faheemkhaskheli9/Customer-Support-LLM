FICTIONAL TRAINING POLICY — NOT A REAL COMPANY POLICY

## Booking an appointment

Appointments at the fictional clinic are booked by phone or at the front desk. The chatbot cannot book, move or cancel an appointment.

## Rescheduling

Please call at least 24 hours before your appointment to reschedule it.

## Late arrival

If you arrive more than 15 minutes late, the clinic may ask you to choose a new appointment time.
