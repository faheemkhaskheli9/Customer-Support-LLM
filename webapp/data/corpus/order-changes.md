FICTIONAL TRAINING POLICY — NOT A REAL COMPANY POLICY

## Changing an order

An order can be changed only before it is dispatched. A support professional must review every change, and the chatbot cannot change or cancel an order.

## Delivery address

To change the delivery address before dispatch, contact support with the order number. The address cannot be changed after dispatch.
