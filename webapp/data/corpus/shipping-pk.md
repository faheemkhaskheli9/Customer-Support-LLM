## Delivery times

We ship to every city in Pakistan. Orders arrive in 3 to 5 business days after dispatch. Deliveries to Karachi, Lahore and Islamabad usually arrive in 2 to 3 business days.

## Delivery charges

The delivery charge within Pakistan is PKR 250 per order. Orders above PKR 5,000 have free delivery.

## Cash on delivery

Cash on delivery is available in Pakistan for orders up to PKR 50,000.
