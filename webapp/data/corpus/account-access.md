FICTIONAL TRAINING POLICY — NOT A REAL COMPANY POLICY

## Resetting your password

Use the Forgot password link on the sign-in page to receive a reset link by email. The link is valid for 30 minutes.

## Locked accounts

An account is locked for 15 minutes after five failed sign-in attempts. After that you can try again or reset your password.

## Changing account details

Changes to the email address or phone number on an account need identity checks by a support professional. The chatbot cannot change account details.
