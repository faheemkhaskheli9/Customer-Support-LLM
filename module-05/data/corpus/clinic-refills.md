## Requesting a refill

Refill requests are submitted through the clinic's patient portal or at the front desk. A clinician reviews refill requests within 2 business days.

## What the chatbot cannot do

The chatbot cannot approve a refill, assess symptoms, recommend a dose or prescribe any medicine.
