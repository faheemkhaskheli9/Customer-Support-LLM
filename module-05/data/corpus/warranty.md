FICTIONAL TRAINING POLICY — NOT A REAL COMPANY POLICY

## Standard warranty

New products have a 12-month warranty against manufacturing faults from the date of delivery.

## Refurbished products

Refurbished phones and laptops have a 6-month warranty against manufacturing faults.

## Making a warranty claim

A support professional reviews every warranty claim and may ask for photos or the serial number. The chatbot cannot accept or reject a claim.
