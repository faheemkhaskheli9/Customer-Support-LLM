FICTIONAL TRAINING POLICY — NOT A REAL COMPANY POLICY

## What the chat remembers

The support chat keeps reported details such as an order number only for the current browser session. They expire after 30 minutes.

## Deleting chat data

Use Delete session data to remove the reported details and the recent conversation from this session.

## What the chat cannot see

The support chat cannot see your orders, payments or medical records. It only knows what you type in this session.
