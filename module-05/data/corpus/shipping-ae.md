FICTIONAL TRAINING POLICY — NOT A REAL COMPANY POLICY

## Delivery times

We ship to all seven emirates. Orders arrive in 2 to 4 business days after dispatch. Deliveries to Dubai and Abu Dhabi usually arrive the next business day.

## Delivery charges

The delivery charge within the United Arab Emirates is AED 15 per order. Orders above AED 200 have free delivery.

## Cash on delivery

Cash on delivery is not available in the United Arab Emirates. Orders must be paid by card when they are placed.
