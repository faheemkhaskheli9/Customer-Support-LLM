# Capstone

Build a small healthcare support assistant that answers approved educational questions with citations, refuses or escalates unsafe requests, and exposes measurable behavior.

Required artifacts:

- Architecture and threat model.
- Versioned source corpus and ingestion report.
- Prompt, model, retriever, and policy configuration.
- Working demo with fake dependencies for tests.
- Evaluation report with failure examples.
- Data-flow, retention, and escalation policy.
- Deployment and rollback runbook.

Definition of done: the system is explainable at the level of source, decision, failure, and owner.
