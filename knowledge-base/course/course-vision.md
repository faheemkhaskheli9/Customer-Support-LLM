# Course Vision

Build practical judgment for customer-support LLM systems: useful enough to help, grounded enough to trust, and observable enough to improve.

The course treats the assistant as a product and socio-technical system, not a prompt. Students move from model fundamentals to a domain-specific RAG assistant, then validate its quality, safety, cost, and deployment posture.

**North star:** every feature has an evidence source, a failure mode, an evaluation signal, and an escalation path.
