# Differentiation

This course is centered on support operations and trust rather than generic prompt tricks. Its distinguishing thread is the full path from customer question to evidence, answer, action, audit record, and human handoff.

It emphasizes:

- Retrieval quality and citation integrity, not just fluent prose.
- Sensitive-data minimization and domain-specific safety.
- Cost and latency as product constraints.
- Failure analysis and evaluation datasets built from real workflows.
- Provider-neutral interfaces so lessons survive model changes.
