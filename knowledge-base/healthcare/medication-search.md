# Medication Search

Use only an approved, versioned medication corpus. Normalize generic and brand names, dosage forms, and spelling variants before retrieval, but do not infer a dose for a person.

Every result should preserve medication name, source, revision date, intended audience, and relevant warnings. A search result is educational context, not a prescription. Questions about interactions, dosage changes, allergies, pregnancy, or adverse reactions should produce conservative guidance and a human or clinician handoff.
